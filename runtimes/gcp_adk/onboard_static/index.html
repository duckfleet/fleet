DuckFleet saves your loyalty profile to your Google account, so only you can see and edit it. Sign in with Google to continue.

Try: “Qantas and Flybuys, no more credit cards, I’d switch electricity for good points, $100 a week, brief me at me@example.com, I’m in Bondi 2026.”

Add DuckFleet to Claude

  1. In Claude, open Settings → Connectors.
  2. Click Add custom connector.
  3. Paste this URL and finish:
https://mcp.duckfleet.dev/mcp

Then in any chat, say “Set up my DuckFleet preferences.” Custom connectors need a paid Claude plan. ChatGPT: Settings → Connectors (Plus and up).
